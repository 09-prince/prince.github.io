Prince Gour

Prince Gour is an AI & Generative AI Engineer who completed his Bachelor's in Computer Applications (BCA) with a specialization in AI & Cybersecurity from Poornima University, Jaipur (2022–2025).

He currently works as an AI Engineer at Shreshtha Consultants, Jaipur (December 2025 – present), where he develops and trains custom AI models to automate MEP (mechanical, electrical, and plumbing) tasks and builds AI-powered tools and intelligent ZWCAD plugins that streamline designers' workflows.

Previously, he was an AI Engineer at NeuroNex Labs (January – November 2025), where he fine-tuned domain-specific Large Language Models using LoRA/PEFT techniques for low-latency inference and managed end-to-end model pipelines, including dataset curation, evaluation, and benchmarking.

His interests include Large Language Models, Transformer architectures, and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF). He is passionate about building AI systems that apply deep learning to real-world NLP and computer vision problems.
